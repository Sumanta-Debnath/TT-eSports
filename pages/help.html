Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

What is the minimum age for beginner training sessions for kids?

We encourage kids as young as six years to get started on their sporting journeys. However, it may vary according to training, which would be explicitly mentioned in the training details.

Do you provide TT equipments when booking a facility?

We always have "test equipment" available in all our facilities. Feel free to make good use of them.

How do I book a trial training class?

We aren't offering any trial classes but feel free to dive right into training worry-free. We only work with highly-rated trainers to ensure trust, quality and safety.

Are all the trainers of TT e-Sports certified?

We tend to play a little bit of a hardball while onboarding trainers and ensure that they have the right certifications. However, certain trainers don't have standard certifications. In that case, we look at relevant coaching experience. If you aren't satisfied with the service of your trainer, please feel free to contact us.

Do I get a full refund if I cancel?

To avoid being on a sticky wicket, make sure you cancel your booking 72 hours prior to the event and avail full refund. The refunded amount shall be credited back to the original mode of payment in 5-6 working days.
